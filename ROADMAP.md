# Roadmap

Features already in the game are in README.md. This file tracks what is planned next, grouped by area.

## Done in the latest update
- Wildlife by biome: rabbit, sika deer, long-tailed monkey, junglefowl, wild duck, sea turtle, rock crab, mountain goat (shown in the collection book once seen; feed them berries)
- Pets: a dog or a cat arrives in a cardboard box on day 3; name it, it follows you, pet it daily
- Livestock: chicken coop and goat/cow barn as town projects; buy animals from Old Bảy, feed wheat, collect eggs and milk
- Seasons (4 x 7 days) with seasonal crops and foliage tint; weather (sun, cloud, rain, storm) with auto-watering rain and a forecast on the board
- Milestones that award licence points; six licences (fishing, farming, mining, stamina, ranching, treasure)
- Museum (Cô Mai) for donating fish and bugs; campfire cooking with seven recipes
- Signature systems: Serenity meter (Bình yên) with perks and an unlockable diary, sit and watch sunrise/sunset/stars, messages in bottles (letters from the city), treasure maps and digging
- Removed Australian-specific elements: eucalyptus became tropical almond (cây bàng), termite mounds became laterite mounds, grass trees became screw pines (dứa dại)

## Next
- Clothing and wardrobe (shirts, hats, colours) sold by a tailor
- Furniture and house interiors, decorating your own house
- Friendship hearts with villagers, birthdays and gifts
- Mines with several floors, ores and gems
- Vehicles: bicycle, small boat to reach remote islets
- Wandering trader with rare items
- Photo mode with a lofi filter and a postcard album of scenic spots
- Festivals each season (lantern night, harvest fair, fishing contest)
- Lofi radio with several stations on a placeable radio
- Hostile or tricky wildlife at night (e.g. wild boar raiding crops) with fences that matter
